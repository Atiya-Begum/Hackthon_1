How the Success Score Is Calculated

IndicatorWeightHow it is measured
Academic Performance30%CGPA converted to a 0–100 scale.
Attendance20%Attendance percentage.
LMS and Assignments15%Assignment completion combined with weekly LMS activity.
Engagement10%Events and campus activities.
Placement Readiness15%Average aptitude, coding and mock-interview scores.
Technical and Soft Skills7%Average technical and soft skills.
Student Feedback3%Feedback rating converted to a 0–100 scale.

Prototype risk rules

  • High risk: academic concern together with low attendance, or score below 45.
  • Medium risk: score below 60, or a key academic, attendance or placement flag.
  • Low risk: none of the above rules are triggered.
Important: These are demonstration thresholds, not validated predictions. Real deployment requires historical validation, appropriate data permissions, privacy safeguards and human review.
